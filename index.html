💡 Reading your results

Download: how quickly data arrives. Upload: how quickly data is sent. Ping: response delay; lower is usually better. Jitter: variation in ping; lower is steadier.

🔐 Privacy

This page does not ask for a Wi-Fi password or store results in an account. Test traffic is sent to Cloudflare. Results can vary with signal strength, your provider, device and network load.
